Args: $ARGUMENTS

1. Read `knowledge/evolve-course/playbook/sops/02-research-by-channel.md` and the research doc structure in `knowledge/evolve-course/playbook/templates/research-doc-structure.md` §2. Read `brands/<brand>/onboarding.md` and `research.md`.
2. If a channel is given, run only that channel's steps; otherwise propose the order of channels and start with the first one. Use the course research prompts from `knowledge/evolve-course/playbook/templates/ai-prompts.md` where the SOP calls for them.
3. Gather real evidence (web search, competitor sites, reviews, Reddit, Meta Ad Library). For competitor ads, a "winner" is an ad ranked #1–2 for its advertiser, never just long-running. Quote customers word for word and keep the source link.
4. Write to `brands/<brand>/research.md` under the doc's sections, and add to the running lists: facts, objections, beliefs, customer words and labels. Date the entry.
5. Summarize what's new and what's still missing, and name the next step (`/product` or the next channel).
